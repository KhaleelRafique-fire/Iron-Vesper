IRON VESPER

How to play:
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
3. Click New Game.

No install is needed. Your save stays in the browser on this computer.

Core controls:
Move: A / D or arrow keys
Jump: Space or Up
Attack: J
Interact: F
Inventory: V
Pause: P or Esc
Mute: O
